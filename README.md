# Elite Recruitment

Elite Recruitment adds two court decisions that let you spend Prestige and gold to
bring a battle-ready commander into your court:

- **Recruit Elite Male Commander**
- **Recruit Elite Female Commander**

## What it does

Crusader Kings II already has a high-end way to gain a strong commander — the vanilla
"promote/employ commander" path, which can even turn up a legendary hero. Elite
Recruitment is meant to deliver that same high-end result, but with a clean, deliberate
**male vs. female** choice instead of leaving the commander's gender up to the game:

- The **male** decision is available to most realms.
- The **female** decision appears for realms whose laws or faith support women in
  command — matriarchal religions, cognatic/enatic succession, high "status of women"
  laws, and pagan faiths.

So you choose the commander that fits your realm, rather than the game choosing for you.

## The recruit

Every commander you recruit is, first and foremost, an **elite tactician**. They're
guaranteed:

- A large **Martial** boost (plus a smaller lift to their other skills),
- The **Brilliant Strategist** education, and
- A **battlefield leadership** trait (defensive leader, flanker, siege leader, etc.).

On top of that there's a **weighted "flavor" roll** that gives each recruit a little
personality — tuned so it *adds* character without changing what they fundamentally are:

- **Most of the time** you get something small and fitting — a touch more Martial, or a
  lifestyle/personality trait like Duelist, Hunter, Strong, or Robust.
- **Occasionally** something bigger or quirkier appears. These are intentionally rare, so
  your recruit almost always reads as "a great commander," not a lottery prize.
- **Rarely**, if you carry a bloodline that *inspires commanders*, your recruit can be a
  **legendary, named hero** — a unique nickname and a stronger set of traits.
- Recruits also pick up **faith-appropriate touches** where they fit (a caste trait for
  Indian religions, a zodiac sign for astrological faiths, and so on).

The baseline is always "a reliably excellent commander"; the random flavor is the
seasoning, never the main course.

## Requirements

- Crusader Kings II `3.3.x`
- **Holy Fury** recommended — the legendary-commander results, faith features, and
  pagan/Indian flavor lean on it. The mod still runs without it; that content just
  won't show up.

## Installation

Copy the `EliteRecruitment` folder and `EliteRecruitment.mod` into:

```
Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings II/mod/
```

…then enable **Elite Recruitment** in the launcher.

## Compatibility, fixes & recommendations

Elite Recruitment works on plain vanilla and adds only new content (it doesn't overwrite
base-game files), so it's friendly with most mods.

**Built-in fixes.** Where vanilla's commander generation has rough edges or out-of-date
bits, this mod uses corrected, current conditions and trait handling — several of them
borrowed from the excellent **CleanSlate** overhaul — so it behaves properly on a modern,
fully-patched game.

**Overhauls & CleanSlate.** CK2 generally loads small add-ons like this *after* a larger
overhaul on its own, and the CK2 launcher has no manual "reorder" — so you normally don't
have to do anything. This mod was built and tested alongside the **CleanSlate** overhaul,
which also fixes a large number of vanilla bugs by itself. CleanSlate is **not required**,
but running it (it's free) is recommended for the cleanest overall experience. If you run a
*different* overhaul and the decisions don't appear, that's a load-order conflict — the
overhaul is replacing the decisions folder and loading after this mod.

## Notes

- The two decisions currently share the vanilla "Employ Soldier" icon as a placeholder;
  custom icons are planned. They'll automatically use a 4K-UI mod's higher-res icon (e.g.
  Proper4KUI) if one is installed.
